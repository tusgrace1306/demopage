Questions, answered

Questions, answered

Does it replace our ATS?+

No. Post the role here and move the candidates you want into whatever you already use.

Who are the recruiters?+

Independent recruiters and agencies in Vietnam, vetted by Inspius. Each one's interview rate and fills are tracked; recruiters whose candidates keep bouncing stop receiving roles.

Does Inspius also submit candidates?+

Yes, like any other recruiter — same rules, same negotiation and no priority.

Who checks the CVs?+

A licensed Inspius recruiter reviews every submission before it reaches you: anonymised, inside your band, the right stack, available. Technical assessment is yours to run — or choose the Recruitment Service and we run it.

What does it cost?+

Each candidate card shows one price — what you pay on hire if you take that candidate. You negotiate it with the recruiter within seven days. Nothing to post, nothing if you don't hire.

How does the price negotiation work?+

The recruiter sets a fee on the candidate. You accept or counter; the recruiter does the same. Seven days to agree, or the submission lapses and the candidate stays with the recruiter.

When do we pay?+

In three equal tranches: first day of work, after one month, after two months. If the hire leaves before a tranche is due, you stop paying. There is no replacement guarantee on the Marketplace; the tranches are the guarantee.

Can two recruiters send the same candidate?+

The first submission wins; the second is blocked. Candidates you hire through the platform are covered by a 12-month ownership clause.

Which roles?+

Software, data and AI roles in Vietnam — Ho Chi Minh City, Hanoi, Da Nang or remote within Vietnam. Not other countries, by design: the recruiter network is Vietnamese.

Can we post roles that specify age or gender?+

No. Listings with age, birth-year or gender preferences are declined at review.

Who is the contract with?+

Inspius Singapore Pte. Ltd. (EA 25S3251) or Inspius Vietnam Recruitment Co., Ltd. (EA 85/2025) — your choice. One contract, one invoice, however many recruiters worked the role.
